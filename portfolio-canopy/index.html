Project Analyst · 2025

OB/GYN Staffing & Burnout

A systems analysis of staffing and burnout at an OB/GYN clinic, part of a larger health informatics research project. Our team of six ran 9 contextual inquiry interviews with clinic staff, and I conducted 6 of them. Together we wrote the scope, recorded each interview’s data in a shared spreadsheet, and presented our findings and recommendations, which we handed off to the next research team.

How I work

  1. 01

    Interview users

    I talk with the people who use the system to learn what they need and where they get stuck.

  2. 02

    Organize the content

    I sort what I learn and arrange the content so people can find what they’re looking for.

  3. 03

    Design and test the screens

    I design the screens in Figma, then run usability tests to see what works and what doesn’t.
